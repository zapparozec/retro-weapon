Saving

Progress is saved automatically when each mission begins. START MISSION on the title screen offers to continue from there. After you finish the game, START MISSION begins a new night from mission 1.

Common questions

  • The game is too hard. Roll through bursts of fire, crouch behind desks and cover, and punch at point-blank range to save ammunition. Checkpoints (posts that turn green) are where you come back; after a game over you may continue three times.
  • What is the story? Choose READ STORY on the title screen: who Detective Nick Reyes is, what happens on the night and how to win. Scroll with the arrow keys, the mouse wheel or the trackpad; Left / Right jump between chapters; Esc goes back.
  • Full screen? Choose TOGGLE FULLSCREEN on the title screen, or use the window's green button.
  • My controller does nothing. Connect it in System Settings > Bluetooth (or by USB) before starting the game, then press a button.
