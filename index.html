PORT MERIDIAN, 1992. ONE COP. ONE LONG NIGHT.

A Syndicate shipment docks at Pier 39 at 02:14 AM. Detective Nick Reyes – long hair, denim jacket, no patience – goes in alone. By 07:00 he will have followed it through every rotten layer of the city to the man at the top.

RETRO WEAPON is a pixel-art action platformer built like the classics: run, jump, roll and shoot your way through six hand-built missions, each with its own place, its own time of night, its own music and its own way of trying to kill you.

SIX MISSIONS, ONE NIGHT

  • HARBOR, 02:14 – rain on the containers, sodium lamps in the fog, and the Foreman guarding the cargo bay with a rotary gun.
  • WAREHOUSE, 03:40 – catwalks, freight lifts and steam in the Syndicate's depot.
  • SEWERS, 05:12 – flooded tunnels, methane haze and the Tideman, who never left.
  • METRO, 05:41 – the Line 3 tunnels as the first trains of the day come through. Signals go green: get into a lit niche, now.
  • CITY, 06:05 – the sunrise on Meridian Avenue: road works in the traffic lane, drive-by bikers, the construction site, the El, the rooftops.
  • HALCYON TOWER, 06:20 – the Syndicate's headquarters, climbed floor by floor: guards, riot shields, men in black suits taking cover behind the desks, security cameras, a stalled lift and a shaft to climb – and the Chairman, waiting in his office.

MOVES THAT MATTER

  • Dodge-roll through gunfire, duck behind office desks, drop through platforms, climb ladders, punch at close range.
  • A pistol with a clip to manage, and a shotgun or an SMG when you can take one off the Syndicate.
  • Chain your kills for a score multiplier up to x8; every mission ends with a tally.

A WORLD THAT FIGHTS BACK

  • Blow up barrels and parked cars, shatter glass, send printers' paper flying with every round.
  • Trains in the tunnels, traffic on the avenue, a crane beam swinging over a building site, an alarm that locks the security floor down.
  • Three bosses who fight dirty: the Foreman, the Tideman and the Chairman – gold pistols akimbo, office chairs kicked at your head, and the whole city below his window.

MADE WITH CARE

  • Every sprite, tile and background hand-crafted in pixel art, lit in real time: rain, neon, flashlights, sunrise.
  • An original 80s synth soundtrack – and one bossa-nova lobby tune you will not forget.
  • Keyboard or game controller. No ads. No in-app purchases. No account. No internet needed.
