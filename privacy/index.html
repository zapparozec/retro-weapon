RETRO WEAPON – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

RETRO WEAPON ("the game") is a single-player game for macOS made by Martins Vanags ("we").

We do not collect any data. The game:

  • does not ask you to create an account or sign in;
  • makes no internet connections – it works entirely offline;
  • contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services, and no third-party code that could collect data;
  • does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.

What stays on your Mac. To let you continue where you left off, the game saves which mission you reached and whether you have finished the game. This is kept only in the game's own preferences on your Mac, inside its sandbox. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the game (or its preferences) removes it.

Children. The game collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes. If this ever changes (for example, if a future version adds online features), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store privacy details will be updated with it.

Contact. Questions about this policy: retroweapon1@gmail.com.
